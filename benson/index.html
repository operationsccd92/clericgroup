The short version

In Shannon Valley, finish level sets the price

Every sale here is a 1980s two-story in the same subdivision, so the houses start out much alike. What separates a $535K sale from a $635K sale is how finished the house is. Dated or lived-in homes sold or are listed between $475K and $588K. Fully renovated homes sold between $599K and $635K, most in one to five days. Benson, finished, belongs in that top group, and it would be one of only two 5-bedroom homes to sell here in the last year.

Scope of work

A straightforward cosmetic flip

  • No foundation issues.
  • HVAC is serviceable. Brand-new tankless water heater already in.
  • No layout changes except opening up the kitchen for an island.
  • Add a full bathroom to the large upstairs bedroom on the right, making it a suite and the house 3.5 baths.
  • Add a closet back to the bedroom behind it, making the 5th bedroom conforming. A small framing job.
  • Replace the walkout basement door.
  • Cosmetics: paint, kitchen, flooring, bathrooms, lighting and finishes to the level of the renovated comps.

Estimated budget: about $80,000.
